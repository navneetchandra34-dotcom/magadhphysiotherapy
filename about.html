Meet Dr. Umesh Chandra

With a career dedicated to restoring functional independence for thousands of patients across the Magadh region, Dr. Umesh Chandra founded Magadh Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Center to bridge the gap between hospital surgery and complete, pain-free daily living.

His clinical methodology integrates thorough orthopedic and neurological diagnostics with individualized manual therapy, spinal decompression, and progressive active rehabilitation—empowering patients to heal naturally and avoid unnecessary surgical interventions whenever possible.

  • Specialized in Complex Spine, Disc & Joint Rehabilitation
  • Advanced Protocols for Post-Stroke & Paralysis Functional Recovery
  • Evidence-Based Sports Injury & Post-Fracture Stiffness Resolution
  • Patient-First Ethics with Transparent, Measurable Recovery Milestones
