Magadh Pain & Paralysis Clinic by Dr. Umesh Chandra provides physiotherapy and rehabilitation services in Motihari, Bihar, with a focus on pain management, movement and everyday function.

Our purpose: help people work towards better movement

Pain, injury, illness and neurological conditions can affect far more than physical movement. They may change how someone manages daily tasks, participates in family life, works or feels about their independence. Rehabilitation can help people work towards practical goals with guidance, practice and review.

Our approach is to begin with the individual's concerns and relevant history, understand how symptoms affect daily life, and discuss a plan that is appropriate to the person's needs. Care should be explained in clear language, with space for questions and realistic expectations.

Physiotherapy is a collaborative process. The clinician brings assessment and rehabilitation knowledge; the patient brings lived experience, priorities and feedback. The aim is to combine these perspectives to identify useful goals, select appropriate interventions and review progress over time.

Principles that guide our work

  • Assessment before assumptions. Symptoms can have different causes, and care should be guided by a suitable clinical assessment.
  • Individualised goals. Priorities may include walking, balance, strength, daily tasks, return to sport or managing symptoms.
  • Clear explanations. Patients should understand the purpose of recommended exercises and what progress may look like.
  • Active participation. Guided exercise, practice and appropriate self-management may form part of rehabilitation.
  • Responsible referral. When symptoms suggest a need for urgent or specialist assessment, that should not be delayed.

What patients can expect

Your first visit may include a conversation about symptoms and medical history, relevant movement or functional assessment, discussion of goals and a proposed plan. The exact process depends on the reason for your visit. You can ask about expected benefits, possible limitations, home exercises, review timing and the circumstances in which the plan should be reconsidered.
