Historical Foundation

Count Cesare Mattei (1809 – 1896)

Electro-Homoeopathy is the name given to a new system of medicine discovered by a famous Italian, Count Cesare Mattei, in the later part of the 1800s.

Count Mattei, the founder of Electro-Homoeopathy, was born on the 11th January in the year of 1809 in Bologna City in Italy. Through spagyric botanical extraction, he developed complex plant remedies designed to purify the blood and lymphatic systems and restore constitutional equilibrium.
