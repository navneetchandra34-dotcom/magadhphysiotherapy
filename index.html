A MORE HUMAN WAY TO REHABILITATE

Progress is personal.
Your care should be, too.

Pain and reduced mobility can change the way you work, sleep, travel and take part in everyday life. Good rehabilitation begins by understanding the person behind the symptoms—not simply the painful joint or affected movement.

At Magadh Pain & Paralysis Clinic, the aim is to help each person understand their condition, participate in a practical treatment plan and track progress with realistic goals.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before
your first visit?

Here are a few practical starting points. For advice about your specific condition, please contact a qualified clinician.

Ask the clinic →
What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring any relevant prescriptions, investigation reports, discharge summaries, a list of medicines and comfortable clothing that allows the affected area to be assessed where appropriate.

Will every condition be treated in the same way?

No. Treatment depends on your symptoms, examination findings, medical history, goals and any precautions. A clinician can explain whether physiotherapy is suitable and whether you need another medical assessment.

How many sessions will I need?

There is no single number that suits everyone. The likely frequency and duration depend on the condition, severity, response to treatment and your goals. Ask for a review plan during your assessment.

Can physiotherapy replace medical care or surgery?

Not in every case. Physiotherapy may be part of a broader care plan, but it does not replace emergency evaluation, diagnosis or other treatment when those are needed. No particular outcome can be guaranteed.

When should I seek urgent medical attention?

Seek urgent medical care for sudden weakness or facial drooping, difficulty speaking, severe chest pain, major injury, loss of bladder or bowel control with back symptoms, or rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not wait for a clinic appointment in an emergency.
