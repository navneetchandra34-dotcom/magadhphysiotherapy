History & Foundation

Count Cesare Mattei
(1809-1896)

Electro-Homoeopathy is the name given to a new system of medicine discovered by a famous Italian, Count Cesare Mattei, in the later part of the 1800's.

Count Mattei, the founder of Electro-Homoeopathy, was born on the 11th of January in the year 1809 in Bologna City, Italy. His revolutionary discoveries laid the foundation for a unique plant-based medical system designed to purify the blood and lymph, targeting the root of diseases.
