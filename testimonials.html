Real Rehabilitation Journeys

Every patient's recovery journey is unique. These examples illustrate how personalised physiotherapy programmes can support improved mobility and quality of life.

🧠 Stroke Rehabilitation

Regaining Everyday Independence

A senior patient experienced weakness after a stroke and enrolled in a personalised rehabilitation programme focusing on balance, walking practice, strengthening exercises and functional movement.

Outcome: Improved mobility and increased confidence in performing routine daily activities through regular physiotherapy.
🦴 Sciatica Rehabilitation

Walking Without Persistent Pain

A patient suffering from radiating leg pain and restricted movement followed a structured programme including manual therapy, posture correction and therapeutic exercises.

Outcome: Better walking tolerance and noticeable reduction in discomfort during everyday activities.
💪 Frozen Shoulder

Restoring Shoulder Movement

Targeted manual therapy and guided mobility exercises helped gradually improve flexibility and reduce stiffness over the rehabilitation period.

Outcome: Improved shoulder movement and greater ease in routine activities.

Returning to Daily Activities
